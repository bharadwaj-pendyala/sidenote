Notes on editing your own writing

So basically, every draft you write is kind of hiding a better post inside of it, and the real job of editing is just finding that post and getting rid of all the stuff around it.

Read it where readers will

Reading your markdown in an editor is not the same as reading the rendered page. Line breaks, headings and code blocks all look really different once they are styled, and problems you never noticed in the source jump out at you on the page.

Comment first, fix later

When you spot something, resist the urge to fix it right away. Leave a note that says what is wrong and keep reading, because fixing as you go breaks your flow and you end up polishing a paragraph that you were actually going to cut anyway.

A short checklist for the second pass:

Cut the throat-clearing at the very start.
Name the thing instead of describing it.
Read it once on your phone.

That is the whole loop: read the page, leave comments, and let the edits come back as a diff you can accept or reject.

npx sidenote-cli dev
